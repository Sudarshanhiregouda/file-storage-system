# On-call rotation policy (fictional)

Sample data for the made-up company "Example Widgets Ltd."

## Rotation
- Each team runs a weekly rotation that starts Monday at 10:00 local time.
- There is always a primary and a secondary on-call engineer.
- Nobody is on call for more than one week in any four-week period unless they volunteer.

## Responsibilities
- Acknowledge pages within 15 minutes, day or night.
- Open an incident channel for any customer-facing outage.
- Hand over open incidents in writing at the end of the shift.

## Escalation
1. Primary on-call.
2. Secondary on-call after 15 minutes without acknowledgement.
3. Engineering manager after 30 minutes.

## Compensation
On-call engineers receive a fixed weekly stipend plus time off in lieu for any page handled between 22:00 and 07:00.

## Swaps
Shift swaps are allowed; update the schedule tool before the swap starts.

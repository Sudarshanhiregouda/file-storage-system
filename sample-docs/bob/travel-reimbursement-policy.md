# Travel reimbursement policy (fictional)

This is a fictional policy for the made-up company "Example Widgets Ltd." It exists only as sample data.

## Scope
Applies to all employees travelling for business purposes approved by their manager.

## Limits
- **Hotels:** up to 150 USD per night in standard cities and 220 USD per night in high-cost cities.
- **Meals:** a daily allowance of 60 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
- **Flights:** economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.
- **Ground transport:** taxis and ride-share are reimbursed when public transport is impractical.
- **Overall limit:** the travel reimbursement limit per trip is 2,500 USD unless a director approves more in advance.

## How to claim
1. Submit receipts in the expense tool within 30 days of returning.
2. Each receipt must show the date, vendor and amount.
3. Lost receipts need a signed declaration and are limited to 50 USD per trip.

## Approval
Claims under 500 USD are approved by the direct manager. Claims above 500 USD also need finance review. Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll cycle after approval.
